Destination

A decision assistant for Council's end-of-pipe litter nets. It reads each day's camera image from SCADA (about 10 working cameras today), scores fill, damage, detachment and can't-see with a confidence, checks the BoM forecast, and emails the duty officer a marked-up photo when a net must be cleaned or repaired before the next storm. The officer approves; a work order follows; the outcome is recorded so each net's fill rate is learned. Other GPT types are shown as a solution map with their own sensors. Nothing is replaced.

Measure of done: the Lackey St West torn net would have produced an email on 13 Nov with an arrow on the tear, on a Saturday. No net detaches unseen; about one email a week.

Not yet specified (the fog)

  • Night / infrared frames: separate model, or "can't see" until dawn?
  • Which Council staff can label sample images (Council offered to explore)?
  • Sediment vs litter: does fullness need to tell them apart for disposal classification?
  • Retraining: does the model learn from each officer approval, and who signs off a new version?
  • Procurement route if Council adopts it: Smart City strategy action, LGP panel, or pilot under the AI Plan?
  • Liability framing for a missed blockage that floods.
  • Multi-council: SSROC and Wollongong run the same contractors and trap types.
  • Success metrics beyond cost: overtopping events, litter mass reaching the river.

Out of scope

  • Buying hardware. Sensors are recommended and simulated, not installed.
  • Replacing or re-platforming SCADA, OneCouncil or CRM.
  • Pipe CCTV inspection (Part F), WSUD maintenance (Part D), water quality sensing.
  • Automated dispatch to the contractor without an officer.
  • Flood prediction. The forecast is an input to the trigger, nothing more.
  • Cost-benefit analysis (Council: not required).

Build order once the questions close

Once the open questions are settled, the build runs in six thin slices. Each one works end to end on its own; the first four are the Festi build.

01
One frame in, one score out

Ingest a frame from the export, run the chosen model, show state + confidence + the frame. frame → score

02
Add rain, get a recommendation

Rainfall feed joined; rule turns score into "urgent / this week / next round / no action". score × rain → action

03
Email goes out, officer decides

Marked-up image, reason, confidence and forecast in one email; approve / defer / inspect; a OneCouncil-shaped work order after approval; audit row written.

04
Damage and can't-see

Torn or detached nets raise a repair email; two days of obstructed frames raise a restore-the-view email.

05
Fullness-at-clean record

Each approved order stores the score at approval and the contractor's reported mass, so the AMP KPI has data behind it.

06
Learn each net's fill rate; other types

Join outcomes to rainfall so each net's mm-to-fill curve sharpens; add fullness sensors for nets without cameras, then racks, pits, vaults, booms.
