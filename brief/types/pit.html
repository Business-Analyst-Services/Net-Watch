Trap in a road-side pit

A basket or insert inside a kerbside drainage pit. Road-side pits across the LGA; no cameras today.

Outside the MVP. Shown so the solution map covers every Council GPT type.

How it fills

Catches litter at the kerb. Small and numerous. A level sensor sees water rising in the pit, which flags a blockage but not how much litter is in the basket.

What the evidence shows in each state

  • No camera view. Fullness is inferred: an abnormal rise in pit level after modest rain points to a blocked basket.

Signal to read: Rate of level rise against rainfall, compared with the same pit's history.

When to email the officer

Email an inspect-and-clear when pit level rises faster than this pit's normal response to the rain that fell. Level is not litter fullness, so the email says 'inspect', not 'full'.
