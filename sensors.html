Layered sensing

Pick the sensor by trap type; keep one decision and email layer

One sensing method won't fit every asset. Cameras work where there is a clear line of sight and enough 4G to send an image. Where there isn't, a low-bandwidth fullness or level sensor sends a number instead. Everything feeds the same rule: is there room for the next storm?

Principles

  • Use what Council has first. About 15 cameras and 25 FloodSmart rain and level gauges already feed SCADA.
  • Bandwidth decides the sensor. Paul's team flagged weak 4G at some nets. A fullness sensor sends a few bytes; an image sends hundreds of kilobytes.
  • A detached net must never be missed. Back the camera with a tilt switch where the view is unreliable.
  • "Can't see" is a state, not "empty". Leaves, fog and night frames raise their own email.
  • Level is not litter. Pit and vault sensors trigger "inspect", never "full".
  • Every clean is a data point. Mass removed and date, joined to rainfall, teaches each trap's fill rate (Paul: "60 mm took it from 10% to 50%").
