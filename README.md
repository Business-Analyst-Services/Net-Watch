# Net Watch

Empty or fix every litter net before the storm.

Net Watch tells City of Parramatta's stormwater officer which gross pollutant traps (GPTs) to empty or repair before the next storm. It reads Council's camera frames alongside the FloodSmart rain and creek gauges, gives every net a priority score, and emails the officer a morning list plus an urgent alert when a net tears. The AI only ranks: an officer approves every job, and every step is logged.

## The facts the site uses

- 37 GPTs across the City of Parramatta, catching about 104 t of litter a year (Council's GPT page).
- 14 cameras on Council's SCADA camera screen, about 10 of them working reliably (Council's estimate), one photo a day each. 11 more sites on that screen show no image.
- Five asset types, with Council's codes and map colours from the December 2022 GPT Audit:
  - **GPT** (purple): end-of-pipe net or basket, the MVP focus
  - **Trash Rack** (blue): steel rack across a waterway
  - **pGPT** (green): trap in a road-side pit
  - **uGPT** (yellow): underground wet vault
  - **Boom** (black): floating boom across a deep waterway
- Priority score: 50% condition, 25% forecast rain against the room left, 15% asset criticality, 10% build-up.

Camera frames and FloodSmart gauges are real. Fill levels, the demo rain forecast, the emails and job history are demo data and are labelled as such in the app.

## Build and deploy

- `python3 build.py` regenerates `docs/brief/`.
- `./deploy.sh` rebuilds, pushes `main`, keeps the latest gauge data, and publishes `docs/` to the `gh-pages` branch, which GitHub Pages serves.

## Model check

`python3 model/baseline.py` reruns the baseline net-state classifier on the labelled frames in `docs/img/frames/` (labels in `model/frames.json`) and writes `docs/data/model-results.js`. It tests leave-one-camera-out over 8 Council frames; simulated frames are used for training only. Result: 1 of 8 correct. This is a small demonstration, not production accuracy, which is why the model only ranks nets and an officer confirms every job.

`python3 model/export_weights.py` trains the same model on all 18 frames and writes `docs/data/model-weights.js` for the in-browser Check a photo tool.

## Site pages

`python3 tools/site_pages.py` writes Home, Pilot plan, Team and Four roles from `tools/pages/*.body.html`, with the shared menu and footer from `tools/sitenav.py` and styles from `docs/css/site.css`. The brief pages use the same menu and footer through `build.py`.
