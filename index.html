Which trap needs the truck this week?

Council cleans its gross pollutant traps quarterly and after big rain, whether they need it or not. The cameras already exist. GPT which GPT? reads every frame, scores each trap's fullness with a confidence, adds the rain forecast, and drafts a work order an officer approves in one click. Start with the type of trap: each one fills differently, shows it differently, and wants a different sensor.

Thresholds are the starting schema; the open ticket "What counts as full?" sets them per type with Council's stormwater team. See simulated examples of the first five states built from two Council frames.

Today

Quarterly rounds plus storm callouts, run by Urban Asset Solutions under Part A of ITT 22/2023 on a schedule of rates. Fullness is judged with the lid off. No per-trap record exists between cleans. Estimated cost ≈ $420k a year, of which $60–120k is visits to traps that were nearly empty.

With GPT which GPT?

Every camera frame scored daily. A ranked morning list instead of a grid of thumbnails. Officer approves, defers or sends someone to look. Work order lands in OneCouncil with the frame attached; fullness at clean is recorded, so the Asset Management Plan's "emptied at optimum frequencies" finally has data behind it.
