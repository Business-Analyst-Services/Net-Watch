GPT type

Pit basket / insert

A lift-out basket under a street grate. Kerb inlets in town centres and shopping strips: Epping, Harris Park, station shops.

How it fills

Catches litter at the kerb before it reaches the pipe. Small, cheap, numerous, emptied by hand or small vacuum unit. Each one is low value; the fleet is not.

What the camera sees

  • Empty: mesh floor visible through the grate.
  • Partial: leaves and cups across the floor.
  • Full: basket heaped to the grate, overflow bypass in use.
  • Blocked: grate itself covered, ponding on the road.
  • Damaged: basket missing, handle broken, mesh split.

Vision cue: Grate-level photo from a phone or a lid camera; the grate pattern gives a fixed reference for fill depth.
