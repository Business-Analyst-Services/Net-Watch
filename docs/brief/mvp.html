What we are demoing

One net, one storm, one email, one decision

A small flow that works end to end beats a broad platform that doesn't. Council said synthetic or sample data is fine and no cost-benefit analysis is needed; the judges score a working prototype and a credible path forward.

Three demo scenarios

01 · CAMERA
Net 75% full, heavy rain forecastAnnotated image, confidence and reason; email says clean before the storm. Officer approves; work order drafted.
02 · DAMAGE
Net torn or off the pipeLackey St West, 13 Nov 2026. Email says repair, not clean, with an arrow on the tear.
03 · DATA QUALITY
Camera can't see, or a sensor is offlineTwo days of obstructed frames raise a restore-the-view email. Never report a false "empty".

What is in and what is out

In

  • Nets, with the other four types shown as a solution map
  • Real Council frames plus synthetic ones, with a simulated fullness sensor feed for nets without cameras
  • Rain-aware trigger from the BoM forecast
  • Email with evidence, and officer approval
  • Outcome recorded, so each net's fill behaviour can be learned

Out

  • Production-grade model training and accuracy claims
  • Live integration with SCADA or OneCouncil
  • Sending contractors out automatically
  • Solving every GPT type
  • Cost-benefit analysis (Council said not required)

Weights from the WSTI Festi judging slide; for City Challenges, community impact includes alignment to Council priorities, community outcomes and implementation potential. Two finalists per city challenge; the jury picks winners at Pitch Night.
