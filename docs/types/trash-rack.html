GPT type

Trash rack / creek screen

Open-channel screens across a creek or culvert mouth. Creek lines through Northmead, Toongabbie, Darling Mills and Hunts Creek; WILLAV 050 is the camera reference frame.

How it fills

Vertical bars or mesh across the flow hold back floating litter and large organics. Material builds up on the upstream face until the screen chokes and water backs up or spills around the ends.

What the camera sees

  • Empty: bars visible top to bottom, bed visible between them.
  • Partial: a band of leaves and bottles along the base, bars still clear above.
  • Full: debris mat stacked two or three layers, bars hidden in the lower half.
  • Blocked: mound over the top rail, ponded brown water upstream, starved channel downstream.
  • Damaged: bars bent, panel sections missing, mat pushed through.

Vision cue: Fraction of bar length still visible, texture of the upstream face, waterline difference upstream vs downstream.

Decision rule

Score from camera; if camera unusable use the level differential; blocked = differential above threshold OR vision ≥ blocked with confidence ≥ 0.8.
