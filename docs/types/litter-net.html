GPT type

Litter net / sock on a pipe outlet

A mesh bag hung on the end of a stormwater pipe. Pipe outlets into creeks and the river: Lackey St East and West, Bettington Rd, James Ruse Dr, Sturt St on the camera wall.

How it fills

Flow carries litter into a net clamped to the outlet; the net swells and sags as it fills. Nets tear on sharp debris and high flows, and a torn net passes everything.

What the camera sees

  • Empty: net hangs slack and translucent, pipe mouth visible through it.
  • Partial: lower third swollen with leaves and packaging.
  • Full: net taut and rounded, sagging to the ground, pipe mouth hidden.
  • Blocked: material backing up inside the pipe, water ponding at the mouth.
  • Damaged: net torn open, frame off its clamps, bag lying on the rocks (Lackey St West, 13 Nov 2026).

Vision cue: Net silhouette area and sag angle, visibility of the pipe mouth, straight edges where mesh has torn.

Decision rule

Score from camera; weight trend confirms; weight drop > 80% in one reading with no clean recorded = damaged.
